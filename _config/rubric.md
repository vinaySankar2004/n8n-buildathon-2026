# Judging rubric

Source: [rules.md](rules.md) and the judging slide. The panel looks mainly at the Devpost page and the demo video, so the video and the page are how every criterion gets scored. Track winners are picked by one person each.

| Criterion | Weight | Judging question (source) | What earns it here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Technical depth and execution | 25% | How complex and well-built is it? Does it actually work? | A real run on camera, start to finish. Real lookups against outside sources, an agent that combines them, handled failures. The n8n canvas and an architecture diagram on the page. |
| Innovation and impact | 25% | Is the idea original, and does it solve a real problem for real people? | A named kind of person with the problem, a sourced number for its size, and why existing tools miss it. |
| Design and user experience | 25% | Is it intuitive, polished, and pleasant to use? | The surface the user touches feels finished. No raw JSON, errors, or debug output on screen. One consistent name and look. |
| Demo | 25% | Did the team clearly show the problem, the solution, and a working product? | The problem lands in the first 15 seconds, the working product carries the middle, and the close says why it matters. |

## Using it

At each stage's human check, rate the stage output 1 to 5 on each criterion and write one line on the weakest. Fix the weakest before polishing the strongest.

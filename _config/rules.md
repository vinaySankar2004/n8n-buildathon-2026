# Event rules

Sources: the organizers' written rules (2026-11-14), the prizes slide and the judging slide shown at the opening.

## Time

- Event: Saturday 2026-11-14 and Sunday 2026-11-15, 09:00 to 18:00, UBC LIFE Building room 2201.
- Hacking: Saturday 12:00 to Sunday 15:00 PST. Nothing is built before Saturday 12:00.
- Submission on Devpost is due Sunday 15:00 PST. Any edit to the Devpost page or the GitHub repo after 15:00 disqualifies the team.

## Eligibility

- Registered, accepted participants. Teams of 1 to 4, one team per person.

## Submission

- Built at the event. Templates, open-source libraries, and public data are allowed.
- n8n track entries must use n8n to do real work, not just run in the background.
- Lawful, public-safe data only.
- Disclose every outside service, model, and template used.
- A demo video of 3 minutes or less is required.

## Prizes

| Prize | Award | Judged by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overall 1st, 2nd, 3rd | $300, $200, $100 | Panel, mainly from the Devpost page and video |
| n8n track | $200 and 4 n8n sweatshirts | Furqan, n8n Campus Ambassador |
| Investly track | $200 and a mystery box (rules); merch goodie bag (slide) | Investly delegate |

Sponsor perks from the slide only, not in the written rules:

- Lovable: 100 credits for every participant.
- ElevenLabs: 1 month Creator for everyone, 3 months Pro per overall winner, 3 months Scale per winner of Best Project Built with ElevenLabs. Whether that award exists and who judges it is open in [judges.md](judges.md).
- Red Bull drinks; Bon Macaron gift cards for 5 winners; Progression Bouldering first-visit passes for 5.

## The event page says something different

The eProjects event page (eprojectsubc.com/event-registration/loop, read on the afternoon of 14 November) differs from the rules handed out at the opening. Until an organizer settles it, plan for both.

- Tracks: AI-enabled workflows, Integrations that remove friction, and Human-in-the-loop. One winner in each track and one overall winner.
- Teams of three to five.
- What you submit: a working n8n workflow, a short demo, and the problem, safeguards and next step.
- Sunday: feature freeze 14:00, submissions lock 14:45, judges visit each team for a short working demo and questions from 15:00, finalists present to the room at 16:05, awards at 17:10.
- Staying after 18:00 is optional and remote; the room closes.

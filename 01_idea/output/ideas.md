# Ideas considered

Brainstormed on the morning of 14 November 2026, before hacking began. The local data newsroom was picked; see [pick.md](pick.md).

| Idea | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Voice money coach, investment scam check, future-you savings voice | Built for the Investly finance track, which we are not entering |
| Club ops autopilot, investor update autopilot, on-call copilot, self-healing n8n, security questionnaire autopilot | Done before, or not niche enough |
| Release notes to launch video | Relano and ngram already do it |
| Voice ops line for restaurant operators | Close second; less use of the team's data and video strengths |
| Lost and found network | A good story, not a fresh idea |
| Matchday picks for sports creators | Too niche for most judges |
| Airline compensation finder | Flight APIs do not report the airline's stated reason for a delay |

## Why the newsroom

It is fresh as a product (automated data stories exist as text; automated local data video is rare), it uses public data the rules allow, and n8n does real work at every step: collecting, scoring, writing, voicing, rendering, and an approval loop.

# Stage: Demo

## Job

Turn the storyboard into a finished video of three minutes or less that shows the real product working.

## Inputs

`output/script.md` (the words), `output/arman.md` (Arman's takes, step by step), `output/runbook.md` (who does what, B3, the edit), `_config/rubric.md`.

## Process

1. Rerun the data pull so the numbers on screen are that day's.
2. Record the golden path for real, phone and screen, beat by beat.
3. Record the narration line by line from the storyboard.
4. Cut to the beats and add captions, since judges may watch muted.
5. Upload only after the team agrees, and write the link down.

## Audit

- [ ] The video is under 3:00.
- [ ] The problem is clear by 0:15.
- [ ] Nothing on screen shows an error, a key or a personal address.
- [ ] It makes sense watched muted.

## Output

`output/link.md`, with the video's link and upload time. The video file stays out of git.

Stop: recorded by Sunday 12:00, uploaded by Sunday 13:30.

# Product discussion

The working product today (D13 to D17): for Rain City Ledger it checks three Vancouver datasets and three news feeds, picks the week's biggest change and drafts a 20 second script. The editor reviews it in a Slack thread, script first and then the video, and replies change either one. Anyone in the editor channel can ask @NewsReels for a video or a number. Every run has a trace, a public "How this story was made" page and a ledger row with its cost.

Settled (D13): the product is NewsReels, a service that turns a client's own sources into short news videos. Clients are newsrooms and individual content creators; the demo client is Rain City Ledger, a made-up outlet. Do not ask about the name again.

Ask one question at a time. Offer two or three concrete options with a recommendation, push for specifics, and record the person's own words.

1. **Clients.** Which client should the pitch lead with: a local newsroom, a neighbourhood newsletter, a solo creator, someone else? What do they do today without it, and would they pay?
2. **How they use it.** Today it is a review thread with replies and two approvals. What else should they be able to do: edit the script, ask for changes in the thread, pick another story from the runners-up, ask about the data, set the schedule?
3. **What it finds.** Sources today: 3-1-1 requests, building permits, business licences, and three news feeds. Which data and news feeds should a client bring? Which story types: biggest rise or drop, records, streaks, one neighbourhood against the rest?
4. **What it makes.** Today: a video, a newsletter blurb, a Slack post. What else: a social post, a chart image, a map, other languages, a weekly digest?
5. **Trust.** Today every run has a trace and a public page. What else would make an editor trust it enough to use it every day?
6. **Demo.** What should the judges remember?
7. **Next three hours.** If you could build one feature before Sunday 11:00, which, and why?

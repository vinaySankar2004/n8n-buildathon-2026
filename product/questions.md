# Product discussion

The working product today: every run checks three Vancouver datasets, picks the week's biggest change, writes a 20 second script, voices it, renders a vertical video, and asks an editor in Slack. Approve posts it with a newsletter blurb to an outlet channel.

Ask one question at a time. Offer two or three concrete options with a recommendation, push for specifics, and record the person's own words.

1. **Name.** What should the product be called, and the made-up outlet whose videos it makes?
2. **Who uses it.** A local newsroom editor, a city communications officer, a neighbourhood newsletter, a councillor's office, someone else? What do they do today without it?
3. **How they use it.** Today it is one Slack message with Approve and Reject. What else should they be able to do: edit the script, pick another story from the runners-up, ask about the data, set the schedule?
4. **What it finds.** Sources today: 3-1-1 requests, building permits, business licences. Which to add, from Vancouver's portal or other cities? Which story types: biggest rise or drop, records, streaks, one neighbourhood against the rest?
5. **What it makes.** Today: a video, a newsletter blurb, a Slack post. What else: a social post, a chart image, a map, other languages, a weekly digest?
6. **Trust.** What would make an editor trust it enough to use it every day?
7. **Demo.** What should the judges remember?
8. **Next three hours.** If you could build one feature before Sunday 11:00, which, and why?
